# Audience analysis

The Analyst's fourth instrument: who the page is reaching and
whether the audience is growing, from page-insights snapshots.
Per-post metrics say what a post did; snapshots say what the
PAGE is becoming.

- Compare the latest snapshot with the previous one, period
  over period, and quote both the absolute delta and the
  percentage. A percentage without its base is a sales trick,
  not analysis.
- Followers are a stock, the rest are flows: report the stock
  level plainly, and judge the flows (views, unique viewers,
  engagements, profile visits) by their direction and size.
- Two efficiency ratios carry the lesson: engagements per 100
  views (is the reached audience acting?) and new followers per
  100 profile visits (is curiosity converting?). Ratios are
  computed in code from the snapshot numbers only.
- A page that posts rarely will show falling flows with a flat
  stock: name the cadence problem when the snapshots show it,
  do not dress a silence as a mystery.
- One snapshot is a level, not a trend: the analysis says so
  until two snapshots exist. Deterministic deltas first, model
  interpretation second, figure-checked as always.
