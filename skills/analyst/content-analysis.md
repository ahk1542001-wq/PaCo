# Content analysis

The Analyst's third instrument: not just how the page did, but
WHICH content did it. Totals hide the lesson; the breakdown by
topic and by format carries it.

- Join every measured post back to its plan row (topic, format)
  before aggregating. A post that cannot be joined is still
  counted, under the topic and format its metrics note records,
  and the join coverage is stated: analysis on partial joins
  must say so.
- Rank groups by engagement rate (engagement per reach), not
  raw totals: a small post everyone acts on teaches more than
  a large post everyone scrolls past. Raw totals are quoted
  alongside so the rate never floats free of its base.
- One-post groups are anecdotes, not patterns: name them as
  the strongest signal available, never as proof.
- The output is a planning instruction, not a eulogy: which
  topic and format lead the next plan, which to rest, and what
  to test once more before deciding.
- Deterministic aggregation first, model interpretation
  second, figure-checked against the computed groups like
  every Analyst output.
