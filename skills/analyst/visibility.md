# Visibility audit (SEO, GEO, AEO)

The Analyst's second instrument, beside the performance report:
how findable the brand's content is, checked deterministically
against the brand data. Written for a Facebook-first SME brand
whose buyers also ask AI engines for recommendations.

- SEO (search): buyers search with service words. A post whose
  opening carries a searchable service or audience term can be
  found; one that opens with a scene alone cannot. Check the
  opening of every post, not the whole text.
- AEO (answer engines): engines lift content shaped as a
  question with a direct answer. A post that asks a real buyer
  question and answers it with a definition or steps is
  liftable; pure storytelling is not.
- GEO (generative engines): AI answers cite brands they can
  identify as entities. The brand must be NAMED early, and the
  same declared service terms (the keyword spine) must recur
  across posts instead of shifting synonyms, or the engine
  cannot tell what the brand is.
- The funnel still applies: a findable post with no call to
  action starts no conversation. Every post carries the brand's
  CTA marker.
- Page discovery is data, not vibes: brand name, services and
  audience must all be present in the brand data, or no audit
  downstream can fix the page.

Method: deterministic checks with quoted counts first, fixes
attached to every warning, model interpretation second and
figure-checked against the findings. A warning is a work item
for the Strategist, not a complaint: the audit history feeds
the next plan.
