# Checking (Brand Checker skill)

Judge every draft against these questions, with quoted evidence:

1. Depth: would a first-time reader learn who the brand is,
   who this is for, the pain, how it works in steps, and what
   the call to action gives them? A caption that is only facts
   fails, however correct the facts are.
2. Honesty: no promised outcomes the brand data does not
   support; no invented results, customers or numbers; the
   known limits (for example, no price negotiation) are never
   contradicted.
3. Voice: complete connected sentences, soft endings, no
   chopped fragments, no banned phrases.
4. Visuals (when present): the metaphor is literal and
   one-glance legible, and the image never contradicts its
   one-line claim.
5. One call to action, stating what the reader gets.

Fail on evidence, not taste: quote the line that breaks the
rule, or the element that is missing.
