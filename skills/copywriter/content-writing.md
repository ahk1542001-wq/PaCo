# Content writing (Copywriter skill)

A caption is content writing, not an information dump. A reader
who has never heard of the brand must finish the caption knowing:

1. Who the brand is and what it actually does, named naturally
   inside the story, never as a slogan pasted on.
2. Who the post is for, in their own daily situation.
3. The real pain, told as a moment the reader has lived, not as
   an abstract claim.
4. How the thing works, in concrete steps the reader can picture.
5. What the reader gets from the call to action, concretely
   (what the free mini-audit shows them, step by step).

Pricing follows the brand's approved data, and the brand data
always wins over this general rule. Where the brand data keeps
prices out of posts (pricing belongs to the conversation that
follows the call to action), prices and costs are never discussed
in content. Where the brand's approved offer publishes its prices
in posts, state them exactly as approved, once and plainly. In
every case a caption never carries a no-pressure disclaimer ("no
obligation", "we won't push you"): it reads as sales talk and
cheapens the post.

Add an experience layer wherever the brand data supports one:
what the team built, tried, or learned. Facts alone never ship.

Length follows the explanation, not a quota: explain clearly and
completely rather than compressing. Short paragraphs, one idea
per line, readable on a phone.

## The client's own voice comes first

When the brand data carries voice samples, paragraphs the client
wrote themselves, they outrank every tone description in this
file. Match their sentence rhythm, warmth, level of formality
and word choices. A brand's voice is learned from the client's
own writing first, and from descriptions second.

## Complete sentences on every platform

Every version, the short ones included, is written in complete,
connected sentences with soft endings. Never chop copy into
blunt fragments: a fragment reads rude in Burmese, and a tight
platform limit means choosing fewer complete sentences, never
broken ones.
