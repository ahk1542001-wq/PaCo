# Copywriting (Copywriter skill)

Copy gives the reader a reason to act now:

- Open on the reader's moment, not on the brand.
- Every benefit must be concrete and checkable: name what the
  system does, step by step, instead of promising outcomes.
- Proof only from the brand data: real work done, real numbers
  from the record. Never invent results, customers, testimonials
  or sales figures.
- One call to action per post, and it must say what the reader
  gets from taking it.
- Honesty is a selling point: state the limits plainly (what the
  system does not do). A reader who trusts the limits trusts the
  offer.
