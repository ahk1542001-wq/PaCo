# Platform variants

One approved master becomes a native post per platform. A
variant is a rewrite for the platform's shape, never a copy
with characters chopped off, and never broken fragments to
fit: fewer complete sentences, always.

- Facebook (master, cap 2000): the full depth version; 8 to 12
  hashtags closing with the brand tag.
- Instagram (cap 2200): the same story, tighter; at most 5
  hashtags; line breaks between thoughts.
- TikTok (cap 2200): written for the video or photo set it
  rides on; the hook is the first line because it is also the
  on-screen text; 3 to 5 hashtags.
- Threads (cap 500): conversational and short; the core claim
  plus one supporting thought; hashtags minimal or none.
- X (cap 280): the core claim in one sentence, one supporting
  clause if it fits, the call to action only if it survives
  without crowding the claim; 0 to 2 hashtags.

Invariants on every platform: the brand facts and the call to
action survive; no price or cost talk ever appears in public
copy; no claim the master did not make; the brand's sentence
rule holds (complete, connected sentences with soft endings).
When a platform toggled off in the brand's connection data gets
no variant: the toggle is the owner's word.
