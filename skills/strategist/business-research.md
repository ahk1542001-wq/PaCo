# Business research

Research comes before brand in the staircase: market,
competition, audience evidence and channels, gathered for the
specific business in the brief. Its output is claims with
sources, and gaps where the evidence does not exist.

- Four kinds, always separated: market (size, shape, spend),
  audience (who buys, their pains in their own moments, how
  they buy), competitor (named rivals, their offer and price
  when sourced), channel (where the buyers actually are).
- Every claim carries its source. A claim the sources do not
  support is recorded as unvalidated, in those words, never
  smoothed into a fact. An invented statistic is the one
  unforgivable output of this stage.
- Counter-signals are evidence too: what buyers rated last,
  what they refuse to delegate, what the data contradicts.
  Record them with the same care as the favourable claims.
- Gaps are a deliverable, not an embarrassment: name each kind
  the sources could not cover, so Brand Builder knows which of
  its levels stand provisional.
- Extract from the gathered items only. The brief frames the
  questions; it is not itself evidence.
