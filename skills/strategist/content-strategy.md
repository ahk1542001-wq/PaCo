# Content strategy (Strategist skill)

Plan from pillars, never from topics alone:

- Each pillar has ONE job in the customer journey (name the
  pain, prove the work, teach, bring the news home, invite
  conversation). A post that serves no pillar does not ship.
- The week is varied on purpose: education, news, engagement
  and proof mixed; promotion is never the centre of the week.
- The brand's repeated messages rotate as the spine of the
  week; every post carries at least one of them.
- One piece per day, written once and adapted across platforms,
  rather than a different piece per platform.
- A news slot stays reactive: its topic is chosen from real
  current news at production time, never invented in advance.
